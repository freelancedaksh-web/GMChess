About GM Chess

The Vision

GM Chess was built with a single goal in mind: to provide a blazingly fast, zero-lag environment for chess purists, blitz typers, and Grandmasters. We are bringing back the raw, unfiltered aesthetic of the late 90s web, stripping away modern bloatware to focus entirely on the game.

Play Globally, Stay Anonymous

We believe in chess without barriers. Our newly integrated real-time multiplayer server allows you to instantly match with opponents from across the globe. There are no accounts, no paywalls, and no tracking. You simply arrive, type your moves, and play pure chess.

Under the Hood

Whether you are playing locally against our WebAssembly (WASM) Stockfish 10 engine or connecting to an anonymous human opponent via our WebSocket architecture, GM Chess operates with near-zero latency. Our algebraic typing interface was strictly designed to handle FIDE notation flawlessly, ensuring competitive players can practice their blindfold and typing speed without interruption.

Open Source & Community

We believe in the open web. Core mechanics and UI elements are available on our GitHub repository. Contributions and feedback are always welcome.
