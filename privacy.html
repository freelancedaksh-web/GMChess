Privacy Policy

Zero Tracking Guarantee

GM Chess respects your privacy to the absolute maximum. We operate a completely anonymous platform. We do not collect personal profiles, utilize tracking cookies, or serve advertisements.

How We Handle Your Data

  • Local Play (vs Stockfish): All engine calculations and move histories are processed entirely within your browser's local memory using WebAssembly. No data leaves your device.
  • Online Multiplayer (WebSockets): When you play against another human online, our server strictly acts as a real-time router. Your IP address is used temporarily to maintain the active WebSocket connection. We do not permanently log IP addresses, nor do we store chat histories or permanent records of your online matches. Once the session ends, the connection data is destroyed.
  • Game Exports: Your game history (PGN) is only generated locally. It is only exported if you explicitly click "Download PGN" or "Analyze on Lichess".

Third-Party Integrations

If you choose to export your game to Lichess via our "Analyze on Lichess" feature, your PGN data will be sent to the Lichess API. At that point, you are subject to the Lichess Privacy Policy.

If you have any concerns regarding data or privacy, please contact our administrative team at: info@gmchess.online
