Privacy Policy

Our stance on privacy is violently simple.

This software operates 100% on the client-side within your browser window. We collect zero telemetry, we utilize zero tracking cookies, and we do not monitor your gameplay.

Because there is no backend server logging your game history, once you refresh the page, your game state is deleted from the universe forever.
