Privacy Policy & Data Compliance

Our commitment to user privacy is absolute and straightforward.

GM Chess operates 100% on the client-side within your browser sandbox. We collect zero personal telemetry, deploy zero behavioral tracking cookies, and store no historical records of your match moves.

Because there is no active server database recording user activity, closing your browser tab or refreshing the window instantly wipes the volatile game state from memory.

Third-Party Services

The application interacts with minimal third-party infrastructure solely for operational integrity:

  • CDNs: Stockfish WASM and chess.js scripts are loaded via secure content delivery networks (cdnjs).
  • Analytics Counter: Real-time aggregate visitor counts are tracked anonymously via CounterAPI without exposing personal identifiers.
